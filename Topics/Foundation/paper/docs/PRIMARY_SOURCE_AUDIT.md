## Conclusion

All ten cited works/resources have matching author/title or institutional metadata and a verified scope relevant to their **actual uses in the paper**. Four substantive original texts were accessible directly at the portions relevant to the paper (Axler, Lubarsky, Gilbert and Lean manual); author-hosted Lyons–Peres text is available, and its bibliographic frontmatter was directly checked. Several publisher-restricted monographs were verified to chapter level through their publisher contents, **not** claim-by-claim from full pages. Since the paper cites them only as background and does not attach external theorem numbers, this limited access does not create an unsupported project-level proof. A fully archival, line-by-line edition audit of the paywalled books remains external to this review.
