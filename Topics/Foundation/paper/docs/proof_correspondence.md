## Post-review precision: representation versus structure (15 October 2026)

- **Signed path integers.** `TreeZ` (`TreeNumbers/PathIntegers.lean`) is a structure of normalized pairs `{(m,n): m=0 or n=0}`, not the primitive Lean `Quot` of `PairEq`. The quotient correspondence follows from `TreeZ.mkZ_sound` and `TreeZ.mkZ_exact`. The paper's display of the difference quotient is a mathematical comparison, not its underlying type.
- **Rational classes and transport.** `TreeQ α` is a quotient of valid path triples. Its operations are lifted via `TreeQ.ofF`, `TreeQ.lift1`, `TreeQ.lift2` from the auxiliary `Frac` field (`TreeNumbers/PathRationalField.lean`), and `TreeQ.orderedFieldIsoFracQ` is structural by construction. The tree-specific statement supplies triple witnesses for all fractions under `[TwoLetters α]`.
- **Ordered-field interface.** `TOField K` (`TreeAnalysis/OrderedField.lean`) extends the project field interface with `Nonneg`, `decNonneg`, cone closure and totality, antisymmetry, and a function `archBound : K → TreeN` with `arch_spec`. This is stronger input than an unspecified abstract ordered field.
- **Constructive quotient nonnegativity.** `TreeR.RNonneg` has quantifier order `∃ c : CSeq K, mkR c = x ∧ (∀ k, ∃ N, ∀ n ≥ N, ...)`. Representative invariance comes from `seqNonneg_resp`/`seqNonneg_of_rnonneg`.
- **Arity comparison order.** `TreeQ.arityIso` is a field isomorphism; the separately proved `TreeQ.transfer_le` (`TreeAnalysis/ArityCompletionTransfer.lean`) supplies order preservation, while reverse transfer yields reflection.
- **Finite decomposition.** `ChildModule.decomposition` is an unconditional identity; `projS_inS`, `decomposition_unique`, and direct-sum conclusions retain positive arity.
- **Unary exceptional values.** The source theorem `TriplePath.unary_numer_ne_zero` excludes zero. The further exclusion of `−1` is proved informally in the revised manuscript from `ne13` and the length equalities; no separate Lean theorem or exact-image characterization is claimed.
