# FINAL AXIOM AND LAW MATRIX

One row per concrete claim about `TreeN`, `TreeZ`, `TreeQ α`, `TreeR K` (and the derived
comparison objects of Task 05).  Generated by `scripts/gen_matrix.py`; rows 1–131 carry over
the Task 04 matrix (`FINAL_PROPERTY_MATRIX.md`) with the Task 05 status vocabulary, rows
132–159 are new.

**Status vocabulary.** `PROVED`: a kernel-checked theorem/definition with exactly the listed
hypotheses. `CONDITIONAL`: proved only under a named, unproved principle that appears as a
hypothesis (`MarkovPrinciple`, `LPO`, `WLPO`, `ChoiceNN`, `SequenceLifting K`, …).
`OBSTRUCTION`: a proved negative result about a *specific* construction or map (not a general
impossibility). `OPEN`: not proved and not refuted in this project (no independence claim).
`DEFINITION_ONLY`: a definition, no proof content.

**Hypotheses.** `[TwoLetters α]` is the typing premise of the carrier `TreeQ α` as a field
(two distinct letters for witness triples); it is discharged by `Child b` for every `b ≥ two`
(`AtLeastTwo b`) and by the omega alphabet. `TreeR` rows hold for every `[TOField K]`, concretely
`K = QStar = TreeQ ChildOmega`; the `Task04.*` certificates are stated for `TreeRStar := TreeR QStar`.
Interface lemmas (`CSR.*`, `TField.*`, `TOField.*`) count for a carrier only through its proved
concrete instance.

**Axiom column.** Union of `Lean.collectAxioms` over the cited declarations, computed by Lean
when this file was generated (`release/declaration_signatures.txt` lists every cited
declaration with its elaborated type and axioms). `[]` means no axiom. The enforcing check
over *all* project constants is `ReleaseAudit/Run.lean`.
