# Finite geometric classification — planned theorems

For star root r and leaves i with pairwise effective resistances R(r,i)=1 and R(i,j)=2 for i≠j, resistance polarization gives `G=I_b` and points `p_r=0`, `p_i=e_i` with `||p_u-p_v||²=R(u,v)`.

All b+1 points are affinely independent, giving an orthogonal b-simplex. The b child points form a regular (b−1)-simplex. Nonempty terminal subsets label simplex faces and face dimension is |B|−1. Keep the boundary tag, since five singleton boundaries in b=4 all have identical zero DtN matrices.

Dimensions to distinguish: child coordinates b; longitudinal line 1; transverse rank b−1; relative whole-star rank b; retained boundary DtN rank |B|−1 for connected star; whole-star affine dimension b; children-only regular facet b−1; full interior-neighbor star with b+1 terminals yields an effective regular b-simplex.

Qualified selection: **if** a 3-dimensional child transverse facet is required, b−1=3 selects b=4. A separate dimension-count condition `d(d−1)/2=d` has nonnegative solutions d=0 or 3; excluding zero gives b=4. Neither creates orientation, wedge-product isomorphism or an intrinsic global three-dimensional manifold.

Positive semidefinite DtN form, positive definite grounded form and resistance Gram identity are different carriers; see [METRIC_FORMS_AND_POSITIVITY](METRIC_FORMS_AND_POSITIVITY.md).
